## About

Clan Head is a business-building company. We help founders, creatives and businesses turn ideas and ambition into functioning ventures by bringing together strategy, operations, brand, communications and technology.

## Our mission

To help founders turn what they envision into something they can actually build, run and grow.

## What we work across

| Area | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Strategy & Insights** | Market research, business strategy, opportunity assessment, growth strategy |
| **Business & Operations** | Business structuring, operational setup, processes, systems |
| **Brand & Communications** | Naming, brand strategy, visual identity, copywriting, content strategy |
| **Digital Growth & Technology** | Websites, e-commerce, apps, CRM, automation, digital marketing |

## How we work

**Discover** > **Define** > **Build** > **Launch** > **Grow**

We start by understanding the founder, the business and what it actually needs, then build the right foundations from there.
